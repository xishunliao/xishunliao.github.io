Multiplayer Human-in-the-Loop Simulation Platform

2019 - 2023 UCR

Project Overview

This platform connects multiple human participants in shared traffic scenarios. It combines SUMO background traffic with Unity vehicle dynamics and interfaces for studying interactions between human drivers and automated vehicles.

Key Features

  • Integration of background traffic, vehicle dynamics, and human controls.
  • Configurable traffic scenarios with driving behavior data collection.
