Research Projects

Methods, demonstrations, and results across three connected research themes.

Human Mobility & Generative Modeling

  • Traveler Digital Twin: Travel Demand Modeling

    Generative models of daily activities and locations, linked to large-scale traffic simulation.

  • Semantic Trajectory Analysis

    Language models and probabilistic inference for classifying places and identifying travel activities.

Human-Centered Driving & Intelligent Vehicles

  • Driver Digital Twin

    Personalized lane-change prediction using a vehicle-edge-cloud architecture and field validation.

  • Driver Profiles

    Modeling relationships among personality traits, mood states, and driving styles.

Transportation Digital Twins & Simulation

  • Multiplayer Human-in-the-Loop Simulation

    A shared simulation platform for studying human behavior and vehicle interactions in mixed traffic.

  • Vehicle Digital Twin

    Vehicle-to-cloud coordination for cooperative ramp merging, demonstrated with real vehicles.

  • Traffic Network Digital Twin

    Data fusion and learning models for traffic impacts around work zones and incidents.
