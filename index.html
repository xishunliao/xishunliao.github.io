My research integrates urban geospatial intelligence, human behavior, and artificial intelligence to model human mobility and develop digital twins for intelligent transportation and autonomous mobility. I study generative models of human activity and travel demand, mobility AI agents and networks, and personalized driving behavior. At UCF, I am affiliated with the Knights Digital Twin Initiative and the Center for Integrated Coastal Research.

I received my Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from UC Riverside in 2023. Before joining UCF, I was a Postdoctoral Scholar and Assistant Project Scientist at UCLA. I serve as an Associate Editor of the IEEE Open Journal of Intelligent Transportation Systems and a Guest Editor of the Journal of Intelligent & Robotic Systems.
